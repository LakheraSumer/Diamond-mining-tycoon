## Observed differences in the superseded revision

- Headwear, goggles, teal/cream/tan/orange colour regions, map pouch and boot lace/sole construction correspond to major reference design features.
- Eye/lid rings read as separate circular facial patches; source almond-shaped eyelids, eyelash mass and cheek integration are not matched.
- Mouth appears flat or mildly downturned, with a more angular jaw and chin than the smiling, rounded reference face.
- Hair uses sparse, similarly sized tubular locks rather than the source dense flowing curl mass.
- Scarf reads as a bulky continuous angular collar; layered fabric wrapping and soft fold transitions differ.
- Jacket shoulders have faceted/jagged silhouette transitions. Animation/deformation inspection is still required before deciding whether joins expose gaps.
- Map fingers lie across the sheet and need a supporting grip/contact correction.
- Garment folds, hardware wear and source-specific garment silhouette remain simplified.

The source camera, lighting and pose were not recovered exactly. These qualitative form/expression observations do not claim measured pixel alignment. Hidden surfaces remain reconstructed. Actual Unity import, all ten clips and Main movement/cart interaction require separate evidence for this revision.

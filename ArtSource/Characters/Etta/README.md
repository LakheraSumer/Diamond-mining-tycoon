# Etta reference and model review

The named original PNG was not found in the project, D:/games or Codex attachments. These crops preserve exact decoded pixels from the available 1024x682 JPEG copy; they cannot certify pixel equality to the missing original PNG.

`01_SourceCrop.png` is the unchanged crop of that available roster copy. `AuxiliarySheet.png` is the supplied generated modelling guidance. The numbered 02–10 images are actual-model review outputs when present, not recovered original assets.

- The original Etta has textured hair, a violet headband/lamp and protective glasses, not a miner helmet. The auxiliary sheet’s headband supports this.
- Keep the original hair gathered upward, apricot shirt, dark trousers and purple-laced boots. Do not replace the distinctive volume with a smooth cap.
- Retain the original raised gem and precision tool pose; added gem-case trays and rear hair geometry are reconstructions.

Side and back surfaces are reconstructed. See `reference.json` for crop coordinates, hashes and source palette samples, and `model-views.json` for actual render provenance.
